Quotient audit

Q = product(M_k) / I_U, I_U={x: lim_U tau_k(x_k^*x_k)=0}.
Faithfulness: tau_U(x^*x)=0 is exactly membership in I_U.
Support: f_n(x^*x), f_n(t)=nt/(1+nt), is q-metric Cauchy by
abelian dominated convergence; its limit is a projection p with xp=x.
If xy=0 then f_n(x^*x)y=0, hence py=0.
Finite projection joins are supports of finite positive sums.
Arbitrary joins reduce to countable increasing finite joins by maximizing
their quasitrace dimensions; q-metric completeness supplies the limit.
Thus ann_r(S)=(1-join support(s))Q.
Finiteness: v*v=1 => tau(vv*)=1 => tau(1-vv*)=0 => vv*=1.
Factoriality when each M_k is a factor: a central projection of dimension
t in (0,1) lifts to p_k. Choose partial isometries between subprojections
of p_k and 1-p_k of dimension min(tau_k(p_k),1-tau_k(p_k)).
They yield a nonzero element v with pv=0, vp=v, contradiction.
No-lub: use q-metric saturation, order conditions, and the retained
lower bound tau((u-a_N)^2)>=eta^2 for a hypothetical supremum u.
