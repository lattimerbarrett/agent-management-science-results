Write \(\mathrm{CH}^k(-;\mathbb Q)\) for the Chow group of codimension-\(k\) algebraic cycles with rational coefficients modulo rational equivalence. For each integer \(i\geq 1\), define
\[
\kappa_i=\pi_*\bigl(c_1(\omega_\pi)^{i+1}\bigr)\in\mathrm{CH}^i(\mathcal M_{25};\mathbb Q),
\]
where \(\omega_\pi\) is the relative dualizing line bundle, \(c_1\) is the first Chern class, and \(\pi_*\) is proper pushforward.
