EXPLICIT 2-COMPLETE EXHAUSTION FOR MIXED-LEVI CANDIDATE

Ω={ρ<0}, ρ=||z'||^2+(|w|^2-1)^2-4, z' in C^{n-1}.
Define ψ=-log(-ρ). It is smooth on Ω. Since Ω is bounded, ρ is continuous
on its closure and ρ=0 exactly at the boundary, ψ is an exhaustion.
Also ρ>=-4, so ψ>=-log 4.

The chain rule gives
Hψ=(-1/ρ) Hρ+(1/ρ^2) ∂ρ tensor conjugate(∂ρ).
For vectors (v',0), its Levi quadratic form is exactly
(-1/ρ)||v'||^2+(1/ρ^2)|sum conjugate(z'_j)v'_j|^2>0
when v'!=0. Thus Hψ is positive definite on an (n-1)-dimensional
complex subspace, so it has at least n-1 positive eigenvalues. Therefore
Ω is 2-complete (with the convention q-complete means n-q+1 positive
Levi eigenvalues).

For n>=3, q=2<n. If one can verify a theorem asserting Oka exterior for
smoothly bounded q-complete domains with q<n, this finishes the higher-
dimensional counterexamples. Together with q=1 for Stein domains, that
same theorem would prove all-dimensional sufficiency. This file DOES NOT
assert existence or proof of that currently unverified exterior theorem.
