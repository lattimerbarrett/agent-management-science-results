# A self-contained strictness proof in rank 3

Use composition phi psi=phi o psi and the row Fox Jacobian J(phi)=(ab(partial phi(x_i)/partial x_j)). The Fox chain rule gives J(phi psi)=J(psi)J(phi) on IA (because IA acts trivially on the abelian group ring). Thus rho(phi)=J(phi^-1)|_(t_1=t_2=t_3=-1) is a genuine homomorphism to GL_3(Z). The Fox fundamental identity shows J(phi)(1,1,1)^T=(1,1,1)^T after evaluation, hence rho induces GL_2(Z) on the quotient by this primitive vector. Coordinates are (v_1-v_3,v_2-v_3).

Let
 A=[[-1,0],[0,1]], B=[[-1,2],[0,1]], C=[[1,0],[2,-1]].
Projectivizing the quotient representation (so central minus signs disappear), the nine Magnus generators in order
 g=(K12,K13,M1;23,K21,K23,M2;13,K31,K32,M3;12)
have images
 (B,A,AB,C,A,AC,C,B,BC).
Here Kij sends x_i to x_j x_i x_j^-1, and M_i;jk sends x_i to [x_j,x_k]x_i. Each remaining basis generator is fixed.

The subgroup generated by A,B,C is their FREE PRODUCT of order-two groups. Proof: on the real projective line, A(z)=-z, B(z)=2-z, C(z)=z/(2z-1). With D_A=(-infinity,0), D_B=(1,infinity), D_C=(0,1), each of A,B,C takes each of the other two domains into its own domain. A reduced word is nonidentity: choose an input domain different from both its first and last letters (possible since there are three), then track the domains from right to left. The final image lies in the first-letter domain, different from the input domain.

Let R be the completed F_2 algebra F_2<<a,b,c>>/(a^2,b^2,c^2), I its positive-degree ideal. The assignments A->1+a, B->1+b, C->1+c define a homomorphism theta from this free product to R^times. The leading highest-degree monomial of a reduced word has coefficient one, so the homomorphism is injective (injectivity itself is not needed below). Its lower central series satisfies theta(gamma_r) subset 1+I^r, by the usual filtered-unit commutator identity.

For homogeneous derivations D_i=tau_1(g_i), let D(ijkl)=[[[D_i,D_j],D_k],D_l]. Exact integer polynomial arithmetic verifies the following identity:
 3 D(0152) -4 D(0111) +2 D(0251) -2 D(1231)=0.
This identity involves only free-Lie derivations on three generators; agent4_seed_verify.py verifies it exactly, coefficient by coefficient (not by sampled evaluations or modulo a prime).

Set W(ijkl)=[[[g_i,g_j],g_k],g_l] and
 w = W(0152)^3 W(0111)^(-4) W(0251)^2 W(1231)^(-2).
Then w lies in gamma_4(IA_3), and the identity gives tau_4(w)=0, hence w lies in A_5(3).

For the target representation, the degree-one parts of theta rho(g_i) are
 (b,a,a+b,c,a,a+c,c,b,b+c).
Modulo I^5, theta rho(w)=1+P, where
 P=abac+abca+abcb+acab+acba+babc+baca+bacb+bcab+bcba+caba+cbab.
Indeed the three correction exponents are even, the leading exponent 3 is odd, and the leading commutator polynomial is [[[b,a],a+c],a+b]. The twelve listed square-free-adjacent monomials are distinct. Thus P is nonzero, and w cannot lie in gamma_5(IA_3).

Propagation is equally explicit. For any nonzero homogeneous P in R of positive degree, choose a support monomial u and a letter z distinct from its first and last letters. The coefficient of uz in Pz+zP is one: the first summand contributes its coefficient in P, and the second summand has all monomials beginning with z. Hence [P,z] is nonzero. Choose a Magnus conjugation generator mapping to the reflection corresponding to z and bracket w with it. Strong centrality of A and of gamma proves inductively an element in gamma_c(IA_3) intersection A_(c+1)(3) whose target has nonzero degree-c term. It is not in gamma_(c+1). This proves STRICTNESS FOR EVERY c>=4 IN RANK THREE.

The same explicit seed and every propagated word belong to gamma_c(IA_n) intersection A_(c+1)(n) under free-factor extension for every n>=3. This alone does NOT prove their nonvanishing in gr_gamma IA_n: stabilization injectivity must not be silently assumed.
