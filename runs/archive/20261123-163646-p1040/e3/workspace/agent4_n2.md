# Rank two and formal reduction

Write i_g for conjugation by g. Nielsen's elementary generation theorem says Aut(F(x,y)) is generated by S=(y,x^-1), T=(x,xy), R=(x^-1,y). The standard Euclidean-algorithm presentation of GL_2(Z) for their abelianizations is
S^4=1, S^2=(ST)^3, R^2=1, RSRS=1, RTRT=1.
The lifts satisfy all relators exactly except S^2(ST)^-3=i_(xy^-1). Therefore the classes of S,T,R in Out(F_2) define a surjective map GL_2(Z)->Out(F_2) inverse to abelianization. Consequently IA_2=Inn(F_2). All the asserted word relations are checked in agent4_verify.py.

We next prove Z(F_n/gamma_(r+1))=gamma_r/gamma_(r+1), n>=2. The inclusion right to left is automatic. For the converse, take g not in gamma_r and let 1<=s<r satisfy g in gamma_s\gamma_(s+1). By the integral Magnus-Witt theorem its leading homogeneous Magnus term P is a nonzero degree-s element of the free associative algebra on X_1,...,X_n. If g is central modulo gamma_(r+1), then [g,x_i] lies in gamma_(r+1), so [P,X_i]=0 for every i. This is impossible: a homogeneous polynomial commuting with X_1 is a multiple of X_1^s. Indeed equality PX_1=X_1P equates the coefficients of X_1u and uX_1. If a support word contains another letter, move its initial X_1 letters to its end using this equality; the resulting support word starts with another letter. Its product with X_1 has nonzero coefficient in PX_1 but zero coefficient in X_1P. Finally X_1^s does not commute with X_2. This proves the center identity (r=1 is immediate).

The center of F_2 is trivial, so g->i_g identifies F_2 with Inn(F_2). Hence gamma_r(IA_2)=i_(gamma_r F_2). Moreover i_g lies in A_r precisely when g gamma_(r+1) is central in F_2/gamma_(r+1), equivalently g in gamma_r. Thus A_r(2)=gamma_r(IA_2) for EVERY r>=1. In particular gamma_c(IA_2) intersection A_(c+1)(2)=gamma_(c+1)(IA_2).

For every n, the Andreadakis filtration is strongly central: [A_r,A_s] subset A_(r+s). One proof uses Magnus expansions: an automorphism in A_r induces a continuous substitution on Z<<X_1,...,X_n>> of the form X_i->X_i+terms of degree >=r+1. As an additive operator, phi-id raises degree by r. Commutators of such operators of filtration r,s raise degree by r+s. Integral Magnus-Witt detection translates this to the asserted group-filtration inclusion. Therefore gamma_c(IA_n) subset A_c(n). The Johnson map A_c/A_(c+1)->Hom(H,L_(c+1)) sends phi to x_i->[phi(x_i)x_i^-1]; it is injective by its definition and the characterization of gamma_(c+2). Its restriction induces a map j_c:gamma_c/gamma_(c+1)->Hom(H,L_(c+1)) with kernel exactly (gamma_c intersection A_(c+1))/gamma_(c+1). Thus the original question is exactly the question of noninjectivity of j_c, integrally, not only rationally.


# Degree one for arbitrary rank

Let H=F_n/gamma_2(F_n), let e_i be the classes of the free generators, and identify L_2=gamma_2/gamma_3 with Lambda^2 H using [x_j,x_k] -> e_j wedge e_k. The map
 t: IA_n -> Hom_Z(H,Lambda^2 H),
 t(phi)(e_i) = phi(x_i)x_i^-1 modulo gamma_3,
is a homomorphism because IA acts trivially on both H and L_2. Its kernel is A_2 by definition.

Use the classical Magnus generating theorem: IA_n is generated by the following n*binomial(n,2) automorphisms:
 C_ij: x_i -> x_j x_i x_j^-1 (i != j),
 M_i;jk: x_i -> [x_j,x_k] x_i (j<k; i not in {j,k}),
with the remaining basis elements fixed. Their t-images are respectively e_i^* tensor (e_j wedge e_i), and e_i^* tensor (e_j wedge e_k). These constitute a signed basis of Hom(H,Lambda^2 H): for each i, every pair contains i (the C type) or does not contain i (the M type), uniquely.

To spell out why this proves the integral assertion, let B be the free abelian group with basis these generators. The generator map induces a surjection B -> IA_n/[IA_n,IA_n]. Composing with t gives an isomorphism B -> Hom(H,Lambda^2 H). Therefore the surjection is itself an isomorphism, as is the induced t. Hence ker(t)=[IA_n,IA_n], i.e. A_2=gamma_2(IA_n), integrally, with no appeal to rational ranks. Thus gamma_1(IA_n) intersection A_2 = gamma_2(IA_n).

For a succinct rank-two final proof, the complete paragraph can be:
Nielsen generation and the usual GL_2(Z) presentation show IA_2=Inn(F_2), as checked by the lift calculation above. For a free group F of rank at least two the center of F/gamma_(r+1) is gamma_r/gamma_(r+1). To verify this, take the least nonzero homogeneous Magnus term P of an element outside gamma_r. Centrality would make P commute with every free associative variable; the center of the free associative algebra in positive degree is zero, a contradiction. Therefore A_r(2)=Inn(gamma_r F_2). Since F_2 has trivial center, Inn is an isomorphism F_2->IA_2, and it preserves the lower central series. Consequently A_r(2)=gamma_r(IA_2) for every r, proving every requested rank-two equality.
