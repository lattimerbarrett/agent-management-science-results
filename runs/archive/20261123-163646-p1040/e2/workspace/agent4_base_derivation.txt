Exact initial Johnson calculation for n=3 base witness.

Write X=X1,Y=X2,Z=X3 in free Lie ring L(X,Y,Z), with usual bracket.
Let D=D1(K12), A=D1(C1;23), B=D1(C3;12), where K12 conjugates
x1 by x2 and C_i;jk multiplies xi by [xj,xk] on either chosen side.
Then
 D(X)=[Y,X], D(Y)=D(Z)=0;
 A(X)=[Y,Z], A(Y)=A(Z)=0;
 B(Z)=[X,Y], B(X)=B(Y)=0.
Set E=[A,B]. By direct derivation rule,
 E(X)=-[Y,[X,Y]]=[Y,[Y,X]]=ad_Y^2(X),
 E(Y)=0,
 E(Z)=[[Y,Z],Y]=-[Y,[Y,Z]]=-ad_Y^2(Z).
Therefore
 [D,E](X)=ad_Y^3(X)-ad_Y(ad_Y^2(X))=0,
 [D,E](Y)=0,
 [D,E](Z)=0.
Thus [D,[A,B]]=0 identically over Z, not just modulo a prime.

Consequently strictness holds for n=3 and every c>=3.
