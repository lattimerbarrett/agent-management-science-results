LEMMA. Every enumeration degree with a Pi^0_2 representative is weakly cototal.

Let K be the ordinary halting set and J=K join complement(K), its total characteristic-set representative. Standard quantifier normalization shows every Sigma^0_2 set C is c.e. relative to K; consequently C <=_e J. Also J =_e complement(K), since K is c.e. and can be enumerated without an oracle, whereas complement(K) is directly a projection of J.

Given A in Pi^0_2, set B=A join K. The c.e. summand is enumeration-computable without an oracle, so B =_e A. Moreover complement(B)=complement(A) join complement(K). The left summand is Sigma^0_2 and therefore <=_e J =_e complement(K). Hence complement(B) =_e complement(K), whose enumeration degree is total, witnessed by J. Thus B witnesses weak cototality of d_e(A).

This proof concerns a REPRESENTATIVE of d_e(A). It does not assert that the literal complement of A has total enumeration degree.
