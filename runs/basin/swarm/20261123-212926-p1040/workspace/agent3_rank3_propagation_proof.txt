For rank3 let W=[[M3;12,M1;23],C12] and L=M1;23*M3;12.
The specialized Magnus/Fox homomorphism at t=(1,-1,1), after transpose
if using right-to-left automorphism composition, has image
H x <-I>, where H=<D,U,V> is the free product of three involutions.
Indeed U,V are opposite elementary matrices with off-diagonal entries +/-2.
Their projective actions have ping-pong domains |z|<1 and |z|>1, proving
projective freeness; D inverts each and has determinant -1. Thus H=F2:C2,
which is C2*C2*C2. Scalar -I is independent and can be projected away.

Put a=D, b=U*D, c=V*D (all involutions). In the mod2 noncommutative ring
R=F2<a,b,c>/(a^2,b^2,c^2), map the three involutions to 1+a,1+b,1+c.
This defines a group homomorphism. For every finite class q, truncate R
above degree q; its units 1+positive-degree ideal have class at most q.
The degree1 terms of U,V,D are a+b,a+c,a respectively. Therefore W has
degree3 term
 P=[a+c,a+b,a]=cba+bca+acb+abc=[a,(b+c)^2].
Here associative brackets in characteristic2 are xy+yx.
The degree1 term of L is s=b+c. Define W_3=W and W_q=[W_{q-1},L].
Inductively its degreeq leading term is ad_s^(q-1)(a).
This is nonzero for every q>=3: in its binomial expansion, the word
 a b c b c ... (q letters total) occurs exactly once, in the endpoint
 a*s^(q-1), and is not killed by the square-zero relations. Thus the
class-q truncated ring detects W_q, so W_q is not in gamma_{q+1}(IA3).

The Johnson degree3 derivation of W is zero: writing x=x1,y=x2,z=x3,
[M3;12,M1;23] sends x to -ad_y^2(x), z to ad_y^2(z), y to0,
which commutes with C12 (x -> [y,x],y,z ->0). Thus W in A4.
The Andreadakis strong central property [A_i,A_j] <= A_{i+j} implies
W_q in A_{q+1} inductively, while W_q in gamma_q(IA3) by construction.
Hence (3,q) belongs to D for EVERY q>=3.

Elementary ping-pong detail (to avoid taking Sanov on trust): choose coordinates
so U(z)=z+2 and V(z)=z/(2z+1), allowing generator inversions if signs differ.
For X={|z|>1} and Y={|z|<1}, every nonzero power U^k sends Y into X,
and every nonzero V^k sends X into Y: |z+2k|>1 for z in Y, and
|z/(2kz+1)|<1 for z in X. The two cyclic factors act freely by the
ping-pong lemma, proving projective freeness. A direct proof of that lemma
is obtained by conjugating any cyclically reduced alternating word so that
it starts with a U-power and ends with a V-power; it sends X into U^k(Y),
a proper subset of X, so cannot be the identity transformation. Pure powers
are visibly nonidentity. Projective freeness also proves that -I is not in
<U,V>, and determinant distinguishes D from <U,V>. Thus the full 2x2 image
has exactly the asserted direct-product/free-product decomposition.
