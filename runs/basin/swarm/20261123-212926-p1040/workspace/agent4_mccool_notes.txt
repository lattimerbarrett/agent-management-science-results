Diagnostic McCool calculation — NOT an IA_n result
====================================================
Let PΣ_3 be the subgroup generated by the six conjugations K_ij,
where K_ij conjugates x_i by x_j and fixes the other generators.
The standard McCool presentation has relators
 [K_ij,K_kj]=1 and [K_ij,K_ik K_jk]=1 for distinct i,j,k.
(The four-distinct-indices relators do not occur for n=3.)

Inn(F3) is normal in PΣ_3. Inner conjugation by x_j equals the
product of the two K_ij with i!=j. These two generators commute.
Modulo Inn(F3), set
 K31=K21^{-1}, K32=K12^{-1}, K23=K13^{-1}.
All the McCool relators become trivial on making these substitutions.
Consequently PΣ_3/Inn(F3) is free on the three remaining generators.
The quotient therefore splits, so PΣ_3 = F3 ⋊ F3.
The action on the inner F3 is induced by IA automorphisms and acts
trivially on its abelianization. Hence this is an almost-direct product.

For an almost-direct product K⋊H of free groups, commutator induction
from [H,K]⊂γ2(K) gives [γ_i(H),γ_j(K)]⊂γ_{i+j}(K).
Thus γ_c(K⋊H)=γ_c(K)⋊γ_c(H) for every c. As abelian groups its
lower central graded quotients are direct sums, so for PΣ_3 their
ranks are twice the Witt numbers for a rank-three free group:
 2/c * sum_{d|c} μ(d)3^(c/d).
In degrees 1..7 these are [6,6,16,36,96,232,624].
