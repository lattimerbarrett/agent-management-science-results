INTEGRAL IMAGE OF THE THIRD LOWER-CENTRAL JOHNSON HOMOMORPHISM
Let V_n=H_n^* tensor Lambda^2 H_n. Let J_3:L_3(V_n)->W_n=H_n^* tensor L_4(H_n) be evaluation as derivations. Let C_3(H_n)=(H_n^{tensor3})/(cyclic rotation differences), a free abelian group on necklaces of length3. Let Tr:W_n->C_3 be left contraction and cyclic projection. Then im J_3=ker Tr for every n>=2.

This is an IMAGE statement only; it DOES NOT prove the third graded Johnson map from gamma_3 IA/gamma_4 IA is injective.

Universal facts: Tr J_3=0, directly from the derivation bracket and cyclic projection. An element of L_3(V_n) is generated by [D,[E,F]]; the divergence identity gives Tr[D,[E,F]]=D Tr[E,F]-[E,F]Tr D=0 since Tr[E,F]=0 and Tr D is linear, so the second summand is the cyclic class of a free Lie word of degree3, which vanishes.

Trace preserves the weight (input-letter counts minus output index). For negative weights it is zero. A positive weight is a degree3 commutative monomial. If its support contains >=2 labels, choose an integral right inverse of the corresponding trace block using only those labels. Such a right inverse exists from the finite surjectivity checks in ranks2,3, since the full map decomposes by weight. For a cube a^3 choose an auxiliary root r!=a and use the rank2 right inverse, with r chosen as1 unless a=1, in which case r=2. Thus we have an integral right inverse q:C_3->W_n, local on <=3 labels, except a cube requiring one auxiliary root.

For each coordinate Lyndon basis column w of W_n, w-qTr(w) belongs to ker Tr. These columns generate ker Tr. A zero-trace w is supported on at most5 labels. A nonzero-trace w has its root among its input letters, hence is supported on at most4 labels; qTr(w) adds at most one root in the cube case. Hence every generator of ker Tr is supported on at most5 labels.

Exact finite certificates in agent1_degree3_image_checks.py prove im J_3=ker Tr in ranks2..5: image ranks2,43,216,705, trace ranks4,11,24,45, and W ranks6,54,240,750. Trace is onto with primitive unit-echelon basis. The image lattice is primitive in the ambient free associative tensor module: its integer row-echelon pivots are only1 or2, yielding a maximal minor that is a power of2; its reduction modulo2 has full rational rank. Thus no prime can divide its Smith torsion. This avoids an unmanageable full dense Smith calculation.

Embedding the local image preimages of the above <=5-label generators proves ker Tr subset im J_3 for all n, completing the proof. The script verifies traces of all integral degree3 image basis vectors vanish; the universal divergence calculation replaces finite tests for that identity.
