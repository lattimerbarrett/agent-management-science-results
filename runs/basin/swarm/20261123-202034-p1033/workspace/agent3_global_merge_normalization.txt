GENERALIZED RANK NORMALIZATION (agent3)

A generalized configuration has t disjoint nonempty cores Xi and nonempty
colored classes Fi of sets Xi union P, with P disjoint Xi and |P|<=di.
The union is intersecting and has covering number t; duplicated edges across
colors are harmless and may be assigned to any source color. No color can
then become empty, since its omission would give a (t-1)-core cover.

The minimum of sum di over generalized configurations equals b(t).
One inequality is immediate. Conversely choose K>max_i(|Xi|+di). Add private
core points so new |Xi|=K-di. For each old petal P of size <di add ALL
(di-|P|)-subsets of a fresh reservoir Z of size max_i di+t. Old full edges
embed in every resulting edge, so intersection remains. Core representatives
cover in t points. A putative cover of <t new points can be normalized by
replacing any new private core point with an old core point of that color;
this does not increase its size or lose any covered member. Reservoir choices
can avoid this cover, since |Z\C|>=max_i di+1. Thus the normalized old-ground
part would cover every old member with fewer than t points, impossible.
All resulting edges have rank K, and deficits are exactly di.

Therefore choose a generalized minimizer of D=b(t), and among those choose
one of minimum total ground size. Merge two points v,w whenever they are not
in distinct cores. Core nonemptiness/disjointness and petal rank budgets are
preserved; intersection is preserved under quotient. For this quotient,
covering number drops iff some old minimum t-cover contains BOTH v,w:
- such a cover quotients to a (t-1)-cover;
- a quotient cover with <t lifts to a cover of at most t old points, by
  replacing the merged point with {v,w}; it must have exactly t-1 points,
  contain the merged point, and lift to a minimum old t-cover containing both.
If covering number did not drop, the quotient would be a generalized
configuration of the same deficit budget but smaller ground size, contradiction.
Hence EVERY pair not lying in distinct cores co-occurs in a minimum t-cover.
This includes all external-external pairs and external-core pairs.

Additional deletion consequence: all core points are used by another color,
except possibly the sole point of a singleton core. Otherwise delete an
unused core point (non-singleton case); all intersections and t-cover property
survive. Every external point and every non-singleton core point has a private
crosspair of full members whose sole intersection is that point: otherwise
its deletion preserves intersection, petals only shrink, and remaining cores
still supply a t-cover. Such a private pair must have distinct source colors.

This is a proved normalization only. It does NOT yet bound ground size or
used core points by O(d^3); that remains the missing lower-bound theorem.
