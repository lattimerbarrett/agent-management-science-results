A simpler upper construction: duplicate center colors.

Let m,n be positive integers with n>4m-1. Put T=nm and r=n-2m.
Let the actual points be the edges of K_n. There are m colors/classes for
each center a in [n]. Give every class i its own disjoint T-point core X_i,
outside all actual points.

For every r-subset P of the (n-1)-edge star at a, let O(P) be the 2m-1
other centers b such that ab is omitted from P. Let L(P) be all m classes
at every center in O(P), so |L(P)|=ell=m(2m-1).
For each class i centered at a, include every set
 X_i union P union {x_j:j in L(P)},
where all choices x_j in X_j are independent. The rank is
 k=T+r+ell=T+n+2m^2-3m,
and every deficit equals D=n+2m^2-3m.

Distinct classes give distinct sets, as one set contains its complete own
T-point core and at most one point of each other core. Every class is
nonempty. All cores are disjoint and nonempty.

Intersection: same-class members share their core. Same-center petals
intersect since 2r>n-1. For different centers a,b, disjoint petals force
at least one to omit actual point ab. If P at a omits ab, it includes
one representative of every class at b, hence intersects the full core
of the other member.

Covering number: selecting one representative of each core gives a
T-point cover. Conversely suppose |C|<T. No core is exhausted. Let
A be C's actual edges, and B the classes whose cores C meets. For each
class outside B, its whole petal family must be met by A, because any
petal disjoint from A can have all its independent target-core choices
made outside C. A hits every r-subset of center a's (n-1)-edge star iff
d_A(a)>=n-r=2m. Thus, writing Z={a:d_A(a)>=2m},
 T-|B| <= m|Z| <= (1/2)sum_{a in Z}d_A(a) <= |A|.
But |C|>=|A|+|B|, a contradiction.

Taking n=m^2, m>=4, gives T=m^3, D=3m^2-3m. For arbitrary large t take
m=ceil(t^(1/3)) and keep any t classes. Every subunion of t classes has
covering number t: a cover of size<t could be supplemented with one
core representative per omitted class to cover the whole original
family in fewer than T points. Therefore
 b(t)<=t(3 ceil(t^(1/3))^2-3 ceil(t^(1/3)))=(3+o(1))t^(5/3).

The asymptotic leading constant from this construction can be optimized:
take n=4m^2, giving T=4m^3, D=6m^2-3m and constant 6/4^(2/3).
