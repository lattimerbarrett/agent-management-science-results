COMPATIBLE ALL-CHOICE NORMAL FORM WITH A RANK-t MEMBER IN EVERY CLASS

Pure separated generalized model. Each class i has a disjoint core X_i;
a base pattern is (P,T), where P is an actual point set disjoint from all
cores and T is a subset of other class labels. Its full members are
X_i union P union {x_j:j in T}, for ALL independent choices x_j in X_j.
Assume |P|+|T|<=d_i<=d, the full family is intersecting, and tau=t.
Suppose t>2d. Initially core sizes can be arbitrary (the usual factor-2
normalization may be used to arrange this model with large cores).

THEN, without increasing any residual cap d_i, one can modify this
model so that all-choice symmetry is retained and:
 (1) t-d_i<=|X_i|<=t for every class;
 (2) each class i contains some ACTUAL rank-t members of form
 C_i=X_i union A_i union {x_j:j in L_i}, for ALL choices x_j in X_j,
 where |A_i|+|L_i|=t-|X_i|<=d_i.
The whole resulting family still has covering number t and is
intersecting. Its other members may have size greater than t.

IRREDUNDANT COVERS. A partially, but not fully, met core contributes
only to covering its OWN class. Indeed, a foreign base pattern has at
most one independently chosen point in that core; a choice outside the
partial cover avoids it. Thus an irredundant cover meets every
nonexhausted core in at most one point.

SYMMETRIC CORE MERGE. Identify two points a,b of X_i, retaining all
independent target choices. Intersection is preserved and no residual
budget increases. The new covering number is either t or t-1, and it
is t-1 iff some old minimum t-cover contains BOTH a,b: map such a cover
to the quotient; conversely lift a new cover of size<t, replacing the
merged point by both preimages if necessary, to get an old cover of
size at most t. By the preceding fact, an old minimum cover containing
a,b must EXHAUST X_i.

LOCKING ALGORITHM. Keep some classes locked. To lock an unlocked i,
merge pairs in X_i while tau remains t, stopping as soon as there is a
minimum t-cover C exhausting X_i. Such a stop must occur: every class
has an edge of size at most |X_i|+d_i, and any edge of an intersecting
family covers it, so throughout |X_i|>=t-d_i>t/2>=1. Hence one cannot
merge forever, and a first forbidden merge supplies C. This cover
exhausts NO other core, since all core sizes exceed t/2. Consequently
irredundancy gives C=X_i union A union one point from each core in L,
with |A|+|L|=t-|X_i|<=d_i.

ALL CHOICE VARIANTS OF C ARE COVERS. Classes i and those in L are met
through their own cores. For h outside L union {i}, an actual pattern
(P,T) must either have i in T or P meet A: otherwise choose its target
points in every partially met core outside C, giving an old member
disjoint from C. This contradicts that C is a cover. This condition
also shows that every alternative choice of the points in L is a cover.
Add all these variants as members of class i. They meet every old
member because they are old covers, and meet each other in X_i.
Their residual size is t-|X_i|<=d_i. Adding members cannot decrease
tau, and any new rank-t member covers the enlarged intersecting family,
so tau remains exactly t. Lock i.

Later merges are only in UNLOCKED cores. A previously added rank-t
member has at most one point in any such foreign core, so its rank
stays t under a merge; all choices remain available and intersection
is preserved. Thus its lock persists. Repeat at most t times. The
finite total ground shrinks between locking steps, proving termination.
No edge can acquire two owners: a class-h edge has at most d_h<=d
points outside X_h, while every other core has >t/2>d points.

USEFUL CONSEQUENCE. For every original/current base pattern (P,T) of
class h outside L_i union {i}, P meets A_i OR i is in T. Thus these
rank-t seeds give short dual certificates as well as pairwise
intersecting actual members. They do NOT by themselves make all old
members rank t, nor prove a binomial or 5/3 lower bound.
