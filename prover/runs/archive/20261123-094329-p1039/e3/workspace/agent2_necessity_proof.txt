Lemma 1. An open set Y satisfying the stated CAP has no bounded continuous
plurisubharmonic function which is nonconstant on a connected component.
Proof. Let u be such a function, bounded above. For every affine holomorphic
map f from a neighborhood of the closed unit disk to Y, CAP supplies entire
F_j:C -> Y converging uniformly to f on that disk. Every u o F_j is a
bounded-above subharmonic function on C, hence constant by the subharmonic
Liouville theorem. Continuity of u, uniformly on a compact neighborhood of
the image disk, gives u o f constant. Small affine disks in arbitrary
complex directions show u locally constant, hence constant on components.

Lemma 2. If an open subset D of C^n has a smooth strictly pseudoconvex
boundary point q, it has a bounded continuous plurisubharmonic function
which is not locally constant.
Proof. Translate q to 0. Choose r with D={r<0} locally, r(0)=0,
dr(0)!=0. Replace r by s=r+A r^2. For large A>0, its complex Hessian at 0
is positive definite: the correction to the Hessian is 2A dr^{1,0} tensor
conjugate(dr^{1,0}), positive in the complex normal direction; the
restriction of the old Hessian to the tangent hyperplane is positive
definite. A block-matrix Schur complement proves the assertion. The sign of
s is the sign of r sufficiently near 0. The second-order Taylor expansion
is s(z)=2 Re g(z)+L(z)+o(|z|^2), with g a holomorphic polynomial of degree
at most two and L positive-definite Hermitian. Shrink B_delta so that
L(z)+o(|z|^2)>=c|z|^2 for some c>0. Consequently
2 Re g(z)<=-c|z|^2 on D intersect B_delta. Thus
h(z)=Re g(z)+(c/4)|z|^2 is smooth strictly psh and
h(z)<=-(c/4)|z|^2 there. Pick 0<eta<c delta^2/16 and use the local function
max(h,-eta) on D intersect B_delta, and the constant -eta on the rest of
D. On D intersect {delta/2<|z|<delta}, h<-eta; hence these definitions agree
on a collar and give a globally continuous psh function u. It takes values
in [-eta,0), equals -eta on this collar, and tends to 0 along D approaching
0. It is therefore not locally constant on D.

Application. For K in K_2, let D=C^2 minus K. If the stated orientation of
Levi pseudoconvexity for K fails at q, its Levi form on the one-dimensional
complex tangent line has a negative eigenvalue. The local defining
function for D is the negative of a defining function for K. Its Levi
form on that line is therefore strictly positive. Lemma 2 contradicts
Lemma 1 if D has CAP. Therefore n=2 belongs to N.

No connectedness of D is assumed or needed; the peak construction is
nonconstant on a single local component. The proof uses only CAP with
m=1. Exact symbolic identities are checked by agent2_necessity_checks.py.
