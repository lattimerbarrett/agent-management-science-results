Strictness proof (all n>=3,c>=3), with standard background inputs explicitly named.

Inputs: Magnus generation and Magnus-Witt free Lie theorem; Bass-Heller-Swan K1(Z[t^+-1])={+-t^k}, K2=(Z/2)^2 generated {-1,-1},{-1,t}; stable Steinberg central extension and Matsumoto field-symbol relations; local Hilbert reciprocity filtration theorem. All other steps below are explicit.

Put X=x1,Y=x2,Z=x3. D=K12, A=C1;23, B=C3;12, with Kij(xi)=xj xi xj^-1 and Ci;jk(xi)=[xj,xk]xi. Let B_k=ad_D^k B in the group and w_c=[D,[A,B_{c-3}]]. This is in gamma_c(IA_n). On the free Lie algebra put T=ad Y. Degree-one derivations satisfy D(X)=TX, A(X)=TZ, B(Z)=-TX. Inductively (ad_D^k B)(Z)=-T^{k+1}X and [A,ad_D^kB] is diagonal on (X,Z), with images T^{k+2}X and -T^{k+2}Z. It commutes with D. Hence the degree-c Johnson image of w_c is zero and w_c lies A_{c+1}.

Use chi:F_n->Z sending x2->1 and other generators0. IA_n preserves chi. For N=ker chi, N_ab is a free R=Z[t,t^-1] module with basis xi, i!=2. IA acts R-linearly since phi(x2)x2^-1 lies N and inner conjugation by N is trivial on N_ab. In honest column-matrix convention on coordinates (x1,x3), images are D=diag(t,1), A=E21(s), B=E12(-s), s=t-1. Other coordinates are fixed for these elements. Therefore B_k=E12(-s^{k+1}) exactly. The full image lies I mod s.

Fix c. If c has an odd prime divisor p, k=c/p and let K/Qp be generated by pi with Eisenstein polynomial Phi_p(1+X^k), so residue Fp, e=(p-1)k, zeta_p=1+pi^k. If c is a power of2, c>=4, put d=c/4 and take pi with Eisenstein polynomial X^{2d}-2X^d+2; then residue F2,e=c/2,and i=1-pi^d. In either case mu_p subset K and C=pe/(p-1)=c. Specialize t=1+pi. The Hilbert pairing is trivial (U_r,U_s)_p if r+s>c, and U_{c+1} consists of pth powers. The induced pairing U1/U2 times U_{c-1}/Uc is perfect; both spaces are 1-dimensional over Fp. Consequently (1+pi,1+pi^{c-1})_p !=1.

Balance determinant by an extra coordinate: g -> diag(g,det(g)^-1). Because SK1(R)=0, this maps into stable E(R). Lift through St(R), specialize to St(K), push central K2(K) through the pth Hilbert symbol to mu_p. Ambiguity K2(R) dies: for oddp it is 2torsion; for p2, -1 is square in K so both {-1,-1},{-1,t} die. Thus there is a genuine hom from IA_n into the mu_p-cover of stable SL(K), lying over principal congruence Gamma1. A finite rank SL_M(K), M>=3, suffices for all matrices; stable field K2 agrees with Matsumoto K2 in such rank.

Filtered-cover lemma: write Gamma_r=ker(SL_M(O)->SL_M(O/pi^r)). For r<=c take F_r full preimage Gamma_r. For r>c let F_r be generated by canonical root lifts x_alpha(pi^r O) and canonical torus lifts h_alpha(U_r), WITHOUT central mu_p. Gaussian collection shows F_r->Gamma_r is isomorphic for r>c: torus symbols involve (U_r,U_r), and opposite-root symbols involve (1+ab,a), with 1+ab in U_{2r}; all vanish. Unique Gaussian normal form then excludes central kernel. The same four root cases show F_r normal in F_1 for r>c and [F_r,F_s] subset F_{r+s} for all r,s>=1: nonopposite roots give product parameter ab; torus-root gives (u^k-1)a; torus-torus gives Hilbert(U_r,U_s); opposite-root Gauss has pivot1+ab in U_{r+s}, with central symbols (1+ab, arbitrary). If r+s>c these symbols vanish (pivot is a pth power); if r+s<=c the assertion is automatic from matrix congruence because F_{r+s} is full preimage. Deep normality lets pair-generator commutator bounds extend to all products. Therefore Q=F1/F_{c+1} has nilpotency class at most c and retains central mu_p injectively.

Word evaluation in Q: let i,j be the module coordinates x1,x3 and ell the determinant-balancing coordinate. D stabilizes to torus h_{i,ell}(t). Set k=c-3. In the coordinate order (j,i), the inner commutator [A,B_k] is the standard opposite-root commutator with a=s,b=-s^{k+1}, ab=-s^{c-1}. Exact Gauss formula is
[x(a),y(b)]=x(-a^2 b/(1-ab)) h((1-ab)^-1) y(ab^2/(1-ab)).
Here d=1-ab=1+s^{c-1}; both root corrections have depth at least c. On commuting with D, these roots go to depth at least c+1 and die in Q. Any central ambiguity in the inner Gauss formula also dies on taking outer commutator. The remaining torus commutator is the Hilbert symbol (t,d)^{+-1}, since the coroots (i,ell),(j,i) have Cartan pairing -1. Thus w_c has central image (1+pi,1+pi^{c-1})_p^{+-1}, which is nontrivial. Since gamma_{c+1}(Q)=1, w_c cannot be in gamma_{c+1}(IA_n).

This proves gamma_{c+1}(IA_n) is strictly contained in gamma_c(IA_n) intersect A_{c+1}(n) for EVERY n>=3,c>=3.
