Metaplectic congruence filtration lemma.
Let K be a nonarchimedean local field containing mu_p, O its valuation ring, pi a uniformizer, and C=p v_K(p)/(p-1). Let 1 -> mu_p -> H -> SL_infinity(K) -> 1 be the central extension obtained by pushing the stable Steinberg extension out through the Hilbert p-symbol. Assume (-1,x)_p=1 (automatic for odd p, and for p=2 when i is in K). Put U_r=1+pi^r O and Gamma_r=ker(SL_infinity(O)->SL_infinity(O/pi^r)).

Use canonical root lifts x_ij(a), and standard torus lifts h_i,ell(u). The field Steinberg relations imply:
(i) root subgroups are additive; nonopposite root commutators have product parameter ab;
(ii) torus-root conjugation is the ordinary root-weight scaling;
(iii) torus commutators are Hilbert symbols, with exponent the integral Cartan pairing;
(iv) opposite-root Gaussian collection has possible central factor a Dennis-Stein symbol, whose Hilbert image is (1+ab,+-a)_p (with harmless inverse/sign according to conventions).
All signs in (iv) are harmless here since (-1,x)_p=1.

Local reciprocity gives (U_r,U_s)_p=1 when r+s>C, and U_{C+1} is contained in K^{*p}.
For r>C, define F_r to be the subgroup generated by x_ij(pi^r O) and h_i,ell(U_r), without adjoining mu_p. Gaussian collection gives F_r -> Gamma_r is an isomorphism: all cross-root pivots are in U_{2r}, hence are p-th powers, so (iv) contributes no center; torus symbols (iii) also vanish. Thus collection is exactly ordinary LDU collection, with unique normal form. In particular F_r intersects mu_p trivially.
For r<=C define F_r to be the full inverse image of Gamma_r.

Each deep F_r (r>C) is normalized by F_1. Verify this on the above generating roots and tori: nonopposite-root and torus-root commutators have parameter in pi^{r+1}; opposite-root pivots are in U_{r+1}, hence p-th powers; torus pairs U_r,U_1 have total depth>C. No central discrepancy remains.
More generally, if r+s>C, the same generator check yields [F_r,F_s] <= F_{r+s}: opposite-root pivots 1+ab lie in U_{r+s} and hence are p-th powers; torus pairs U_r,U_s pair trivially. If r+s<=C, the inclusion follows just from the ordinary matrix congruence commutator inclusion, as F_{r+s} is the full inverse image. Therefore (F_r) is an N-series.

Consequently Q=F_1/F_{C+1} is nilpotent of class at most C, and its central mu_p is still injective. All assertions hold in each finite rank >=3 and compatibly under stabilization. Q itself can be infinite-rank; each finitely generated image is a finite p-group since its projection is a congruence quotient over the finite ring O/pi^{C+1}.

CAVEAT FOR FINAL WRITEUP: relation (iv) and Gaussian uniqueness must be presented from the usual Steinberg/Dennis-Stein identities rather than assumed unexplained. The argument above gives the exact valuation verification once those standard identities are stated.
