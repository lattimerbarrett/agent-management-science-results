# Finite-action lower bound criterion (audit document)

Let T=F_3<X_1,X_2,X_3>/(words of length >=9), with unit, and let U be
the finite group of algebra automorphisms X_i -> X_i+terms of length >=2.
For g in F_3 (the FREE GROUP of rank three, not the field), substitute
x_i=1+X_i and x_i^{-1}=sum_{k=0}^8(-X_i)^k. Every IA_3 automorphism
therefore induces a well-defined element of U. This is a group
homomorphism (or anti-homomorphism under the opposite composition
convention, which does not affect any lower central subgroup).

Let Q be the image, generated by the nine actual Magnus generators.
An element in gamma_4(Q) has displacement f_i of wordlength >=5.
Two such automorphisms compose by ADDING their displacements:
substituting one displacement into another first contributes length
5-1+5=9 and hence vanishes in T. Consequently gamma_4(Q) is an
abelian group of exponent three, identified with an F_3-subspace of
3 copies of the wordlength 5..8 component of T.

Write L_g for the exactly linear action on this displacement space
induced by conjugation g^{-1}(-)g, and N_g=L_g-I. The image gamma_4(Q)
is the smallest subspace containing the displacements of all positive
left-normed fourfold generator commutators and invariant under all L_g.
To justify the normal-generation assertion, in any group generated by S,
gamma_c is normally generated by the simple left-normed commutators of
length c in S: induction, since imposing that those length-c commutators
commute with S makes gamma_c central and kills gamma_{c+1}.
Forward invariance under L_g suffices because these are invertible maps
of a finite subspace.

Once V=gamma_c(Q) is contained in this additive displacement space,
 gamma_{c+1}(Q)=sum_g N_g(V).
This sum is already invariant: each N_h(N_g(V)) is contained in N_h(V),
since V is invariant. Thus no extra group collection or p-power closure
is required in degrees >=4.

In particular, if exact computation establishes
 dim_F3 gamma_4(Q)=a, dim_F3 gamma_5(Q)=b,
then gamma_4(Q)/gamma_5(Q) is elementary abelian of rank a-b.
The natural epimorphism IA_3 -> Q induces an epimorphism
 gr_4(IA_3) -> gamma_4(Q)/gamma_5(Q).
If the integral degree-four Johnson map were injective with image rank r,
then gr_4(IA_3) would be free abelian of rank r, and hence a-b<=r.
This needs NO assumption about saturation of the Johnson image.
