# Rank two and the structural reduction

Use composition f g = f after g. The classical Nielsen generator theorem says Aut(F_2) is generated by permutation, inversion, and elementary multiplication of a free basis. Set S=(b,a^{-1}), T=(a,ab), R=(a,b^{-1}); these generate the Nielsen generators. The classical presentation

GL_2(Z)=<S,T,R | S^4=1, (ST)^3=S^2, R^2=1, RSR=S^{-1}, RTR=T^{-1}>

has these abelianization matrices. Exact reduction of free words, checked in rank2_certificate.py, gives S^4=R^2=RSRS=1, (ST)^3 S^{-2}=Inn(ba^{-1}), RTRT=Inn(a). Thus the presentation gives a surjection GL_2(Z) -> Out(F_2), inverse to abelianization. Consequently IA_2=Inn(F_2).

For n>=2, Z(F_n/gamma_{c+2})=gamma_{c+1}/gamma_{c+2}. Indeed Magnus--Witt identifies gr_gamma(F_n) with the free Lie ring embedded in the free associative ring Z<X_1,...,X_n>. A positive-degree homogeneous associative polynomial P commuting with X_1 is a multiple of X_1^d: coefficients of a word w beginning in a different letter vanish by comparison in PX_1=X_1P, and initial X_1 letters can be cyclically moved to the end without changing coefficients. Commutation with X_2 forces that multiple zero. If g not in gamma_{c+1}, choose r<=c with g in gamma_r minus gamma_{r+1}; if [g,F_n] is contained in gamma_{c+2}, its nonzero initial form would commute with all X_i, contradiction. The reverse inclusion follows from the definition of gamma.

Since F_2 is centerless, conjugation identifies IA_2 with F_2 and gamma_c(IA_2) with Inn(gamma_c(F_2)). By the center formula, Inn(g) belongs to A_{c+1}(2) iff g belongs to gamma_{c+1}(F_2). Hence gamma_c(IA_2) intersect A_{c+1}(2) = gamma_{c+1}(IA_2) for every c.

For arbitrary n, work in the holomorph F_n semidirect Aut(F_n). The three-subgroup lemma, by induction on t, gives [A_r,gamma_t(F_n)] contained in gamma_{r+t}(F_n): for the induction step apply it to (A_r,gamma_t,F_n) modulo gamma_{r+t+1}; the other two iterated brackets vanish by induction and [gamma_i,gamma_j] contained in gamma_{i+j}. Applying the same lemma to (A_r,A_s,F_n) modulo gamma_{r+s+1} gives [A_r,A_s] contained in A_{r+s}. Thus gamma_c(IA_n) is contained in A_c. Define J_c on gamma_c(IA_n) by J_c(phi)(x mod gamma_2) = phi(x)x^{-1} mod gamma_{c+2}. It is a homomorphism into Hom(H,L_{c+1}) and vanishes on gamma_{c+1}(IA_n). Its induced graded map has kernel exactly (gamma_c(IA_n) intersect A_{c+1}) / gamma_{c+1}(IA_n). Thus the question is exactly whether this integral graded Johnson map has nonzero kernel in each bidegree.

Dependencies used here: Nielsen generation and the displayed modular-group presentation; Magnus--Witt's theorem. The novel theorem requested in ranks >=3 and degrees >=2 is not proved by these classical facts.


## Degree one in every rank

By Magnus generation, IA_n is generated by K_ij (i!=j) and C_i;jk (i,j,k distinct,j<k). Their degree-one Johnson images are a signed permutation of the integral basis of Hom(H,L2): K_ij gives e_i* tensor [Xj,Xi], C_i;jk gives e_i* tensor [Xj,Xk]. The free abelian group on these generators maps surjectively to IA_n^ab and then to Hom(H,L2); its composite is unimodular. Both maps are therefore isomorphisms. This proves A2=gamma2 in every rank. The indexing proves this for arbitrary n; degree1_certificate.py additionally checks exact truncated Magnus expansions for n=2..12.

# Integral degree-two Johnson injectivity in every rank

## Inputs

Use the proved degree-one identification IA_n^ab=V_n=Hom(H,L_2), with signed basis a_i;jk=e_i^* tensor [X_j,X_k], j!=k. For i in {j,k} its lift is a signed basis conjugation, otherwise a commutator transvection. Let q:Lambda^2 V_n -> gr_2(IA_n) be the surjective bracket map and B:Lambda^2 V_n -> Hom(H,L_3) the derivation bracket, so B=J_2 q.

The exact primitive-lattice certificates degree2_certificate.py prove J_2 injective for n=3,4; rank2 follows from rank2_proof.md. Consequently any z supported on at most four coordinate labels with Bz=0 has qz=0 in every rank: embed the corresponding smaller free-group automorphism group by fixing the extra generators. No injectivity of this group inclusion on lower-central quotients is required.

## Reduction of individual columns

The degree-one basis is multihomogeneous with weight e_j+e_k-e_i; hence Lambda^2 V is a direct sum of integral weight groups and B preserves weight. This is an algebraic multigrading; no assumption that weight-space decomposition follows from the integral diagonal torus is made.

Consider a wedge of two basis derivations. With different targets and no target occurring in the other's output pair, their Magnus lifts commute exactly. With the same target, if both are commutator transvections, their wedge is a true degree-two relation in all ranks by the partial-inner identity proved in agent4_degree2_relations.md. Every remaining column has at least one cross-contraction and its union support has at most five labels. A column supported on exactly five labels has two distinct-target commutator transvections and exactly one contraction; all five labels are distinct and it has form a_i;pk wedge a_p;qr, up to swapping factors/signs. The integral dummy-exchange relation from agent4_degree2_relations.md replaces it by a_i;qk wedge a_q;qr, supported on four labels. Thus every original column is, modulo true relations in ker q, zero or a column supported on at most four labels.

We now canonicalize each resulting column using only relations supported on at most four labels. It suffices to do this weight by weight. The possible nonzero weights in Hom(H,L_3) are (I) exactly one coefficient -1, all others nonnegative; (II) 2e_a; (III) e_a+e_b, a!=b. For any other weight B is zero and every reduced column is a local relation, hence q-zero.

## I: One negative coefficient

Write the weight as -e_i plus three positive letters with multiplicity, none equal to i. The output space is the corresponding multihomogeneous component of the free Lie ring on these positive letters, at target i. If the three letters are distinct a,b,c, an integral basis is [[a,b],c], [[a,c],b]. Both are images, up to sign, of local columns a_i;a c wedge a_a;a b and a_i;a b wedge a_a;a c. If the letters are a,a,b, the output is rank one, generated by [a,[a,b]], which is the image up to sign of a_i;a b wedge a_b;a b. Three identical letters give zero. Choose these columns as canonical lifts of a free basis.

For any reduced column, express its image in this integral basis. Subtract the corresponding canonical lifts. The difference is supported on at most four labels: the actual weight support has size four in the distinct-letter case and size three in the repeated-letter case, while the reduced column has at most four labels and contains the actual weight support. Its image is zero, so local injectivity proves the required equality in gr_2. The canonical images are independent. Therefore J_2 is injective on this weight.

## II: Weight 2e_a

The codomain weight has basis T_i=e_i^*[[X_i,X_a],X_a], i!=a. Every K--K column of this weight consists of two conjugations by X_a and commutes exactly. Every remaining nonzero column has form E_ip=a_i;p a wedge a_p;i a, with i,p distinct from a; its image is -T_i+T_p. Fix any anchor j!=a and take E_ji as canonical star columns, i!=a,j. Their images T_i-T_j are independent. The difference E_ip-(E_jp-E_ji) has zero image and is supported on {a,i,p,j}, at most four labels; terms with repeated indices are interpreted as zero and skew signs. Therefore local injectivity expresses every column in these independent star columns. J_2 is injective on this weight.

## III: Weight e_a+e_b, a!=b

For s outside {a,b}, put U_s=e_s^*[[X_s,X_a],X_b] and V_s=e_s^*[[X_s,X_b],X_a]. Put Z_a=e_a^*[X_a,[X_a,X_b]] and Z_b=e_b^*[[X_a,X_b],X_b]. These form an integral codomain basis. Use the actual columns

W_s=a_s;s a wedge a_s;s b,
L_s=a_s;a b wedge a_a;a s,
G=a_a;a b wedge a_b;a b.

Their images are respectively U_s-V_s, U_s+Z_a, and -Z_a+Z_b. These images are independent and form the full integral kernel of the functional sending every U_s,V_s to +1 and Z_a,Z_b to -1. This follows directly by solving coefficients at U_s,V_s, then at Z_a,Z_b; equivalently the corresponding minors have gcd one.

This functional is the symmetric trace on this weight. Define trace on an associative expansion of D(X_t) by contracting its first letter with t and symmetrizing the remaining two letters. It gives

Tr(e_t^*[[u,v],w])=delta_tu vw-delta_tv uw,
Tr(e_t^*[u,[v,w]])=-delta_tv uw+delta_tw uv.

For D=a_i;jk and E=a_p;qr, the bracket is delta_iq e_p^*[[j,k],r]+delta_ir e_p^*[q,[j,k]]-delta_pj e_i^*[[q,r],k]-delta_pk e_i^*[j,[q,r]]. Its trace is delta_iq(delta_pj kr-delta_pk jr)+delta_ir(-delta_pj qk+delta_pk qj)-delta_pj(delta_iq rk-delta_ir qk)-delta_pk(-delta_iq jr+delta_ir jq), which cancels termwise in the symmetric square. Thus Tr([D,E])=0 integrally, in all ranks. The script agent4_trace_certificate.py verifies this exactly for every pair of degree-one basis derivations through n=8 (41,250 pairs), as well as all W_s,L_s,G signs. The displayed formulas give the general proof independently of these finite tests.

For a reduced column supported on T, with |T|<=4 and {a,b} subset T, its trace-zero image therefore has a unique integral expression in G and those W_s,L_s for s in T minus {a,b}. Subtract that expression from the column. The result has zero image and support within T, so local rank<=4 injectivity makes it a true relation. Thus all columns are expressed in the globally independent G,W_s,L_s. J_2 is injective on this weight.

## Conclusion

Every integral weight has zero Johnson kernel, hence J_2:gr_2(IA_n)->Hom(H,L_3) is injective for every n>=2. Equivalently gamma_2(IA_n) intersect A_3(n)=gamma_3(IA_n). Combined with the degree-one theorem A_2=gamma_2, this proves A_3(n)=gamma_3(IA_n) for all n.

This establishes only c=2 of the requested all-rank low-degree range. It does not establish c=3,4,5 or any high-degree noninjectivity.
