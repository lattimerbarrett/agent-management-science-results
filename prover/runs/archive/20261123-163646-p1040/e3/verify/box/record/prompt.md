For each integer \(n\ge2\), let \(F_n\) be the free group on \(n\) generators. For any group \(G\), define its lower central series by \(\gamma_1(G)=G\) and \(\gamma_{r+1}(G)=[\gamma_r(G),G]\), where \([H,K]\) is the subgroup generated by the commutators \([h,k]=hkh^{-1}k^{-1}\) with \(h\in H\) and \(k\in K\). For every integer \(r\ge1\), define
\[
\mathcal A_r(n)=\ker\!\left(\operatorname{Aut}(F_n)\longrightarrow\operatorname{Aut}\bigl(F_n/\gamma_{r+1}(F_n)\bigr)\right),
\qquad \operatorname{IA}_n=\mathcal A_1(n).
\]
Determine, by explicit conditions on \(n\) and \(c\), the complete set
\[
\mathcal D=\left\{(n,c)\in\mathbb Z_{\ge2}\times\mathbb Z_{\ge1}:\ 
\gamma_{c+1}(\operatorname{IA}_n)\subsetneq
\gamma_c(\operatorname{IA}_n)\cap\mathcal A_{c+1}(n)
\right\}.
\]

The answer is \mathcal D=\{(n,c)\in\mathbb Z_{\ge2}\times\mathbb Z_{\ge1}:n\ge3,\ c\ge4\}.
