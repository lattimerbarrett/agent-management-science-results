AUDITED LOCAL-K2 / HILBERT LEMMAS FOR FINITE-RING PROOF

1. Stable K2 over local rings (precise classical input).
If A is a commutative local ring with residue field of size >=7, the map
K2^M(A) -> K2(A) is an isomorphism (the 5-fold-stable local-ring form of the
Dennis-Stein/van der Kallen presentation theorem). K2^M has generators
{a,b}, a,b in A*, bilinearity and {a,1-a}=1 whenever both are units.
The >=7 hypothesis implies5-fold stability: each unimodular pair forbids
at most one residue value of a parameter, and five pairs forbid at most5.

Let I be an ideal contained in the maximal ideal. From this presentation,
K2(A)->K2(A/I) is onto, and its kernel is generated by {1+x,b}, x in I,
b in A*. Indeed all units of A/I lift to units of A; the bilinear relations
lift, and every Steinberg relation lifts since unit conditions on a,1-a
are detected modulo the maximal ideal. Imposing unit identifications
(1+I)=1 on the tensor presentation produces exactly the stated kernel.
This argument applies to A=O_L and B=O_L/pi^c, with the same residuefield.

2. Local Hilbert symbols (precise classical inputs).
For a finite extension L/Q_p containing mu_p, the p-Hilbert symbol gives
a homomorphism H_p:K2(L)->mu_p. Write U_a=1+pi^a O_L, e=v_L(p), and
h=p e/(p-1). Then H_p(U_a,U_b)=1 ifa+b>h. If0<a<h andp doesnot dividea,
the induced pairing U_a/U_{a+1} x U_{h-a}/U_{h-a+1} ->mu_p is perfect
as a residuefield trace pairing. In particular, when the residuefieldis
F_p, any two nonzero classes pair nontrivially.
For p2 andmu4 subsetL there is H4:K2(L)->mu4 withH4(z)^2=H2(z).
On an unramified extension L/L0 of degreef, Hilbert symbols of elements
fromL0 are raised to thef-th power (Brauer invariants restriction rule).

Since every unit is a Teichmuller unit times a U1 unit, and the Teichmuller
part has order prime top, it is a p-th power. Thus H_p(U_h,O_L*)=1:
use a+b=h+1>h on U_h x U1. This is the exact descent fact needed.

3. Construct the character of K2(B).
Choosec>=3. If an odd primep dividesc, putell=p,r=c/p.
Otherwisec is a powerof2>=4: putp=2,ell=4,r=c/4.
P(X)=Phi_ell(1+X^r) is monic Eisensteinatp ofdegreee=r phi(ell), so
L0=Q_p[X]/P has uniformizerpi, residueF_p, containsmu_ell via1+pi^r,
and h=pe/(p-1)=c. Enlargeunramifiedbydegreef as follows:
f=3 forp2; f=2 forp3 or5; f=1 forp>=7. Thenf isprime top andp^f>=7.
TakeL thisextension, O=O_L,B=O/pi^c. Symbols below use the samepi fromL0.

Oddp: restrictH_p toK2(O). It kills relativeK2(O,pi^c) by1&2, so descends
to chi:K2(B)->mu_p. It kills all2-torsion already onK2(O).
P2: define chi_O=H4 modulo mu2. It kills relativeK2(O,pi^c), because its
squareH2 is1 there, soH4 values lie inmu2. Thus it descends to
chi:K2(B)->mu4/mu2. Also chi_O kills every element oforderdividing2:
itsH4 image is then inmu2.

The symbol xi={1-pi^{c-1},1+pi} has chi(xi) nontrivial. In oddcase this
is boundaryHilbert atindices(c-1,1) overL0, hence nontrivial; unramified
extensiondegreef preserves it. In even caseH2(xi)=-1 by the samecritical
pair overL0 andodd extensiondegreef, henceH4(xi) has order4 and remains
nontrivial modulo mu2.

4. Killing the source K2 is an ORIGIN-sensitive assertion in the even case.
For R=Z[T,T^-1], Bass-Heller-Swan and regularity ofZ give
K2(R)=K2(Z) directsum K1(Z)=(Z/2)^2. Ring mapR->O sendsT to1+pi, and
R->B factors throughO. Every element ofK2(R) maps to an order<=2 element
ofK2(O), killed bychi_O, and therefore chi kills its image inK2(B).
Do NOT merely argue 'chi targethasorder2 so it kills2-torsion' (false).
The factorization viaK2(O), followed by H4 modmu2, is essential.
