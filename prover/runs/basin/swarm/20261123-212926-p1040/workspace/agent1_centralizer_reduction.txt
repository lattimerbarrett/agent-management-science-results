CENTRALIZER REFORMULATION OF THE REQUESTED SET
For n>=2 let G=IA_n and L=gr^gamma G. There is an injective graded Lie map i:Lie(H_n)->L induced by inner automorphisms. Its image J is a graded Lie ideal. The homogeneous Johnson kernel K_c=ker(L_c->Hom(H_n,Lie_{c+1}(H_n))) equals C_L(J)_c, the degree-c part of the centralizer of J. Hence D={(n,c):C_L(J)_c!=0}.

Proof. Standard commutator calculus gives [A_r,A_s] subset A_{r+s}, hence gamma_r G subset A_r. For all n>=2, an inner automorphism inn_w belongs to A_r iff the image of w is central in F_n/gamma_{r+1}, iff w belongs to gamma_r F_n. (The center of a free nilpotent group of rank>=2 is its final lower-central term.) If inn_w for w in gamma_r F_n belongs to gamma_{r+1}G, it lies in A_{r+1}, so w lies in gamma_{r+1}F_n. This proves injection on the graded groups. Inn(F_n) normal in G proves J is an ideal.

The exact identity [alpha,inn_x]=inn_{alpha(x)x^{-1}} holds with the commutator convention of the question. Thus for alpha in gamma_c G and x a free generator, its bracket with i(X) is i(tau_c(alpha)(X)). Since i is injective, alpha's degree-c class centralizes i(H_n) iff tau_c(alpha)=0. The ideal J is generated as a Lie algebra by i(H_n), so by Jacobi, centralizing i(H_n) is equivalent to centralizing all J. This proves the equality.

Consequences. K is a graded Lie ideal and K intersect J=0 (the center of a free Lie algebra of rank>=2 iszero). If the center of L were known to be zero, nonzero K_c would imply nonzero K_{c+1}, because L is generated by degree1: a nonzero K_c element not central must have a nonzero bracket with some degree1 generator. But proving center(L)=0 cannot be replaced by center(G)=0, and is not supplied here. Thus upward propagation of D needs a separate source-side theorem.

The Johnson target is free abelian, so torsion in L_c gives a nonzero element of K_c. Conversely it is NOT proved that all K_c is torsion; that would be a rational Andreadakis assertion.
