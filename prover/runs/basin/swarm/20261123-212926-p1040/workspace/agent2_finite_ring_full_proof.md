# Finite-ring replacement of the metaplectic construction

This eliminates the Gauss-section/local-congruence lemma and global reciprocity entirely.

## Standard K-theoretic/local inputs, precise scope

(1) For R=Z[T,T^{-1}], stable K2(R)=K2(Z) direct-sum K1(Z)=(Z/2)^2, by the fundamental theorem for regular rings. More precisely, K2(Z) is generated by {-1,-1}, while the Laurent summand is the cup product with [T]; the generator -1 of K1(Z) therefore gives {-1,T}, up to convention inverse. Thus these two symbols generate K2(R). The stable Steinberg extension St(R)->E(R) has this central kernel. Functoriality holds for all ring homomorphisms.

(2) If O is a complete DVR with residue field of cardinality at least 7, and B=O/pi^c, then unit-symbol Milnor K2 agrees with stable K2 for O and B. The reduction K2(O)->K2(B) is onto and its kernel is generated by {1+pi^c a,b}, a in O, b in O^*. This follows immediately from unit-symbol presentations: every unit lifts, and every Steinberg relation {x,1-x}=1 lifts because x and 1-x are units iff their residues are nonzero.

(3) For a local field F containing mu_p, absolute ramification e, C=pe/(p-1), its p-Hilbert pairing has (U^a,U^b)=1 for a+b>C, and the critical pairing at a+b=C, p not dividing a, is perfect in the Kummer graded pieces. For residue F_p and a=1 both pieces have dimension one, so two nonzero coefficients pair nontrivially. Hilbert symbols commute with unramified restriction: on elements from the base field the new symbol is the old one raised to the extension degree. The square of the fourth Hilbert symbol is the quadratic symbol.

## Local ring for each c>=3

If c has an odd prime divisor p, put ell=p and r=c/p. Otherwise c is a power of two >=4; put p=2, ell=4, r=c/4. Put

  P(X)=Phi_ell(1+X^r).

This is monic Eisenstein at p of degree e=r*phi(ell), since Phi_ell(Z) modulo p is (Z-1)^phi(ell), and P(0)=p. A root pi defines a totally ramified local field F0/Qp with residue Fp, containing zeta_ell=1+pi^r. Its critical conductor C is exactly c. The symbol

  (1-pi^(c-1),1+pi)_p

is nontrivial by the critical pairing: indices c-1 and 1 are prime to p, leading coefficients -1 and 1, residue field Fp.

Take an unramified extension F/F0 of degree f prime to p and with residue cardinality >=7 (f=3 for p2; f=2 for p3 or p5; f=1 for p>=7). Nontriviality persists by restriction. Write O=O_F and B=O/pi^c.

For ALL p, let lambda:K2(O)->mu_p be the p-Hilbert symbol. It kills ker(K2(O)->K2(B)): every b in O^* is a Teichmuller unit times a principal unit; its prime-to-p-order Teichmuller part is a pth power, and for the principal unit use (U^c,U^1)_p=1. Consequently lambda descends to chi:K2(B)->mu_p.

The boundary symbol {1-pi^(c-1),1+pi} has nonzero chi. The image of K2(R), under T->1+pi, is killed by chi. Indeed its two generators are {-1,-1} and {-1,T}, and -1 is a pth power in F: for odd p use -1=(-1)^p, and for p2 use -1=i^2. Thus both symbols have trivial p-Hilbert value.

## Canonical nilpotent detector

Push out St(B)->E(B) by chi to obtain central extension 1->mu->Etilde(B)->E(B)->1. Functoriality and vanishing of chi on K2(R) give an actual homomorphism

  E(R) -> Etilde(B).

For the ideal J=(pi) in B, J^c=0. Therefore the fixed-rank congruence matrix group Gamma(J)=ker(SL_d(B)->SL_d(B/J)) has nilpotence class <=c-1, because [1+M(J^a),1+M(J^b)] subset 1+M(J^(a+b)). Its preimage in Etilde(B) has class <=c. The center mu survives tautologically in the pushout.

## Fox map and witness (all n>=3)

Let j=2, i=1, k=3; set t_j=T, other abelian variables=1. The abelianized Fox row Jacobian is an antihomomorphism on IA_n. Its transpose is a homomorphism preserving the cycle module with basis indexed by l!=j. The fundamental Fox identity proves invariance over the domain R: the j-coordinate of every cycle-column is zero because T-1 is nonzero. Restrict to obtain rho:IA_n->GL_(n-1)(R). Append determinant inverse as an extra coordinate 0. This gives a homomorphism to SL_n(R), and actually to E_n(R): every Magnus generator is either identity, an elementary root x_ab(+(T-1)) or x_ab(-(T-1)), or a diagonal h_a0(T). Every image is identity modulo T-1. Thus the preceding homomorphism yields a homomorphism from IA_n to a group of class <=c.

Use Magnus generators

 A=M_1;23 : x1 -> [x2,x3]x1,
 B=M_3;12 : x3 -> [x1,x2]x3,
 D=C_32 : x3 -> x2*x3*x2^-1.

Their determinant-augmented Fox matrices are

 rho(A)=x_31(s), rho(B)=x_13(-s), rho(D)=h_30(T), s=T-1.

Define X_3=A, X_(c+1)=[D,X_c], and W_c=[[X_c,B],D]. Equivalently X_c=ad_D^(c-3)(A). Then X_c has source weight c-2 and Fox matrix x_31(s^(c-2)) EXACTLY. Hence W_c lies in gamma_c(IA_n).

For its Johnson term, put t=ad(x2) in the free Lie algebra. The three derivations fix x2 and every other coordinate, and act linearly on x1,x3 as

 A:x1->t*x3, B:x3->-t*x1, D:x3->t*x3.

Thus X_c:x1->t^(c-2)*x3. Its bracket with B is the diagonal derivation (x1->t^(c-1)*x1, x3->-t^(c-1)*x3), commuting with D. Therefore tau_c(W_c)=0 and W_c belongs to A_(c+1).

Finally compute in St(B), modulo its central kernel. Put a=pi^(c-2), b=-pi and v=1+ab=1-pi^(c-1). The 2x2 matrix commutator [x_31(a),x_13(b)] is exactly h_31(v) in B: offdiagonal errors a^2b and ab^2 vanish since their valuations are >=c, as does a^2b^2. Thus any Steinberg lift of this commutator differs from h_31(v) only by a central element. Any lift of D differs from h_30(1+pi) only by a central element. Taking their commutator eliminates all such central discrepancies and gives

 W_c -> {v,1+pi}^((e3-e1,e3-e0)) = {v,1+pi},

up to the harmless overall symbol convention inverse. The Cartan pairing is ONE (not two) because 0,1,3 are distinct. Its chi value is nontrivial. Therefore W_c is not in gamma_(c+1)(IA_n), since the detector has class <=c.

Hence every n>=3,c>=3 belongs to D. The independent elementary n2 and c1 exclusions and integral all-rank c2 certificate finish the classification.
