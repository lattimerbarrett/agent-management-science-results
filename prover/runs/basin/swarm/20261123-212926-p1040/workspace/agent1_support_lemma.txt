BOUNDED-SUPPORT LEMMA FOR THE SECOND JOHNSON BRACKET
Let H_n=Z^n, V_n=H_n^* tensor Lambda^2 H_n, W_n=H_n^* tensor L_3(H_n), and B_n:Lambda^2 V_n -> W_n the derivation bracket. Let T_n:W_n -> Sym^2 H_n be left tensor contraction followed by commutativization. Then:
(A) T_n B_n=0.
(B) If im B_m=ker T_m for 2<=m<=4, then im B_n=ker T_n for all n.
(C) Under the same assumption, ker B_n is generated, under coordinate inclusions, by the kernels ker B_m for 2<=m<=6.

PROOF. Use the ordinary Lyndon basis of L_3 and coordinate tensor bases everywhere. Each basis column of T_n is zero or plus/minus a single commutative quadratic monomial. This follows directly by expanding [[a,b],c] or [a,[b,c]]: after left contraction with i and symmetrization, the two contributions from i=c (respectively i=a) cancel, while either remaining term is a single monomial. Coefficients have magnitude1.

For each monomial ab with a<b select a distinguished coordinate column d_ab with trace ab: take output a, Lie word [[a,b],a], changing its sign if necessary. The Lie word is plus/minus a Lyndon basis element. It has support {a,b}. For square aa select output r and word [[r,a],a], where r=1 unless a=1, in which case r=2. This also is plus/minus a Lyndon word with trace aa.

An integral basis E of ker T_n consists of all zero-trace coordinate columns and, for every non-distinguished nonzero-trace coordinate column w with trace epsilon*ab, the difference w-epsilon*d_ab. Every member e of E is supported on at most4 labels: a coordinate W column uses at most4 labels, and if its trace is nonzero it uses at most3 labels (output must occur among the three inputs); subtracting d_ab adds at most1 label.

By the rank<=4 hypothesis, each e has an integral B-preimage supported on its own labels, padding to2 labels if needed. (There are no nonzero W columns on1 label.) Choose such a preimage and thereby define an integral section s:ker T_n -> Lambda^2 V_n. This proves (B). Assertion (A) is the direct derivation-bracket calculation, also independently finite-checked in agent1_support_checks.py.

For any coordinate basis column u=D wedge E of Lambda^2 V_n, its support S uses at most6 labels. If B_n(u) is nonzero, S uses at most5 labels, since nonzero substitution requires the output index of one derivation among the two inputs of the other. In the canonical ker T basis expansion of B_n(u), all non-distinguished coordinate columns use S. Distinguished off-diagonal trace preimages add no labels. Distinguished square preimages collectively add at most one new label: if 1 is not in S, they can add only1; if 1 is in S, they can add only2. Thus sB_n(u), and hence k_u=u-sB_n(u), use at most6 labels. If B_n(u)=0, then k_u=u already has support<=6. Each k_u lies in ker B_n.

Finally if z=sum a_u*u belongs to ker B_n, then z=sum a_u*(u-sB_n(u)), because sB_n(z)=0. Thus all ker B_n are generated by the k_u on at most6 labels, proving (C).

APPLICATION TO IA. Let R_n be the kernel of Lambda^2(IA_n^ab) -> gamma_2 IA_n/gamma_3 IA_n, using the first Johnson identification IA_n^ab=V_n. Then R_n is contained in ker B_n. If exact source certificates show R_m=ker B_m for m<=6, coordinate inclusions imply ker B_n subset R_n for all n, by (C). Hence the degree2 Johnson map is injective for all n, and (n,2) is excluded from D for all n. Agent2 has source certificates in ranks3..6; rank2 is classical IA_2=Inn(F_2). Exact im B_m=ker T_m for m<=4 is separately required and follows from the bracket ranks + surjectivity of T and primitivity (can verify unit-pivot image matrices).

The companion script does exact tensor arithmetic for n=2..7, verifying T-column form, distinguished preimages, T*B=0, and the nonzero bracket support bound. It is corroboration of the universal combinatorial proof, not a substitute for source relation certificates.
