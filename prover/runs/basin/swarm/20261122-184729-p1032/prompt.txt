\[
\begin{aligned}
I=(&bdfhz,adfhz,acfhz,acehz,acegz,bcegz,bdegz,bdfgz,\\

\]
For an ideal \(J\subset R\), define
\[
\operatorname{Ass}_R(R/J)=\{\mathfrak p\subset R:\mathfrak p\text{ is prime and }\mathfrak p=(J:v)\text{ for some }v\in R\setminus J\},
\]

Determine the complete function \(k\mapsto\operatorname{Ass}_R(R/I^k)\) for all \(k\geq1\). Give an explicit piecewise formula in which every prime ideal is specified by its generators, rather than leaving any collection of minimal primes unevaluated.
