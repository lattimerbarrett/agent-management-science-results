REVERSE BAD-COALITION GADGET (PURE HALL MODEL ONLY)

INPUT: a pairwise-intersecting k-uniform clutter B on W, k>=2, with M
members. Put T=|W|. This constructs T colored actual-petal families of
rank at most M+1 with Hall transversal inequalities. It DOES NOT by
itself construct an intersecting original core family: no suitable
small out-target assignment is supplied.

Take a fresh point v and pairwise disjoint blocks Q_F of size k-1,
one for each F in B. For every x in W define H_x by two types:
 A_x = all sets choosing exactly one point of each Q_F with x in F;
 B_x = all sets {v} union a choice of one point of each Q_F with x notin F.
Use the ordinary convention that a product over no blocks is {empty}.

EXACT ACTIVATION. For any actual point set S, let E={F: Q_F subset S},
and write U=union(E), I=intersection(E), with U=empty,I=W if E=empty.
A_x is entirely hit by S iff x in U. If v in S, B_x is always hit;
otherwise B_x is entirely hit iff x notin I. Consequently the set of
colors whose WHOLE H_x is hit is U if v in S, and U\I otherwise.

HALL. For h=|E|, |U|<=h(k-1)+1 because the inner members intersect.
Also |U\I|<=h(k-1): if I is nonempty, remove its s>=1 points from each
member and use |U\I|<=h(k-s). If I is empty, expose members successively.
Every new member meets the previous union. At the FIRST step when the
running common intersection becomes empty, the new member meets the
previous union in at least TWO points: if its only union point were p,
its intersection with every earlier member would force p into their
common intersection. This gives |U|<=k+(h-1)(k-1)-1=h(k-1).
Thus when v in S, |S|>=1+h(k-1)>=|U|; when v notin S,
|S|>=h(k-1)>=|U\I|. In either case activated colors <=|S|.
Equivalently, for every J subset W, tau(union_{x in J} H_x)>=|J|.

PRUNING v. Delete all petals containing v, leaving exactly A_x.
A coalition J is now bad iff its union of colored petal families has
transversal number <|J|. A cover S activates U as above, and costs at
least h(k-1). If |J|>|S| and J subset U, then |U|<=h(k-1)+1 forces
J=U, |J|=h(k-1)+1, and J contains some inner member F. Conversely, if
F belongs to B, exhausting its single block Q_F, of cost k-1, covers
all k colors of F. Thus F itself is bad. We do not need or assert that
every superset of F is bad; badness need not be upward closed here.
Hence every bad coalition contains an F in B, and each F itself is
bad. The inclusion-minimal bad coalitions are EXACTLY B.

RANK: A_x has degree deg_B(x), B_x has degree 1+M-deg_B(x), both <=M+1.
The large M cost is precisely why this gadget currently does not yield
an efficient recursion for b(t). Replacing blocks by shared factored
cores/petals must reprove activation-Hall, as well as furnish original
core-family intersection; neither follows from this generic gadget.
