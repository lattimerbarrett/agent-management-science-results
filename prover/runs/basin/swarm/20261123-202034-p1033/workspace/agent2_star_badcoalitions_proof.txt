Point-pruning bad coalitions in the regular-owner star construction.

Let G be an h-regular graph on n centers. Each owner color is an edge
ab in E(G). Its petal family consists of all r=(n-h-1)-subsets of
St(a) minus {ab}, and all such subsets of St(b) minus {ab}, where actual
points are E(K_n). Adjoin a private core label c_i to every petal of
color i; assume r>=1. Original colored Hall follows by degree charge.

Fix an actual point v=xy, and remove all petals containing v. For an
actual set S not containing v, let J(S) be colors whose entire remaining
petal family is met by S. Exactly,
 ab in J(S) iff
 deg_S(a)-1_{ab in S} >= h-1_{a in {x,y}, ab != v},
 deg_S(b)-1_{ab in S} >= h-1_{b in {x,y}, ab != v}.
(The formulas follow from the transversal number of all r-subsets of a
q-point set being q-r+1.) In particular old Hall plus one removed point
gives |J(S)|<=|S|+1.

A color coalition I becomes bad precisely when some actual S has
|I intersect J(S)|>|S|, since all other colors in I can be covered by
one private core label each. Consequently every minimal bad coalition
is J(S) with |J(S)|=|S|+1.

For such S let W be endpoints of J(S). Every a in W has
 deg_S(a)>=h-1_{a in {x,y}}.
Thus
 2|J(S)| <= h|W| <= 2|S|+2.
The assumed equality |J(S)|=|S|+1 forces equality throughout. It follows
that x,y are in W; J(S)=E_G(W); every G-neighbor of any W-vertex is in W
(so W is a union of whole connected components of G); all S-edges have
both endpoints in W; and degrees in S are h except at x,y, where they
are h-1. No edge of G can belong to S, since any activated own edge in S
would raise a required endpoint degree above these forced values.

If v belongs to G, its entire component is in W, so v itself is an
activated owner. But this owner's threshold remains h at x and y,
contradicting their forced S-degrees h-1. Thus there are NO bad coalitions.
If v does not belong to G, every minimal bad coalition contains every
owner edge of x's component (and y's component). Therefore any single
color in x's component meets every bad coalition. The minimum number
lambda_v of colors to discard to restore Hall is at most 1.

This proves lambda_v=0 for owner actual points and lambda_v<=1 otherwise,
for every h-regular G where the construction is defined. No assertion
of existence of bad coalitions in the latter case is needed.

Independent exhaustive checks for h=2, G=2K_{2,2}: all actual S of size
at most 7 were enumerated for each of the 3 actual-point symmetry orbits.
Owner v: no bad coalition; nonowner v in one component and across two
components: the sole minimal bad coalition is all 8 colors, lambda_v=1.

by agent_4 via all 256 color coalitions and SAT cover calculations.
