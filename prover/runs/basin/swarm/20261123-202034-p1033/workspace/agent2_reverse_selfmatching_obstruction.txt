RAINBOW SELF-PETALS OBSTRUCT A TARGET-COMPLETION OF FACTORED REVERSE

Let an inner family H be partitioned into T classes with disjoint cores
X_i. Choose one anchor a_i in each core. In the factored reverse gadget,
label the private Q_i (size |X_i|-1) by X_i minus {a_i}; write q_{i,x}.
Each residual point x in an inner member of class j is copied as p_{j,x},
with these copies shared WITHIN owner j and disjoint across owners.
Thus Q_F=Q_owner union {p_{owner,x}: x in F minus X_owner}.

For every nonanchor corepoint x in X_i minus {a_i}, form
 S_x={q_{i,x}} union {p_{j,x}: j!=i and some F in H_j contains x}.

CLAIM: S_x is an inclusion-minimal transversal of
 {Q_F: F in H and x in F}. Hence S_x is an actual TYPE-A petal of the
 reverse color x.
Proof: for F of owner i, its Q_i contains q_{i,x}. For F of any other
 owner j containing x, its residual copy contains p_{j,x}. Conversely,
 every chosen point of S_x belongs to one private owner-ground block.
 That owner block has some relevant F, and S_x has exactly ONE point
 in that block, so this F witnesses indispensability of that point.
This proves both hitting and minimality.

All the S_x are PAIRWISE DISJOINT for distinct nonanchor x: their Q_i
labels and residual-copy labels differ. Thus the reverse gadget has a
rainbow actual-petal matching of size
 q=sum_i(|X_i|-1).

Consider ANY proposed intersecting-core realization that keeps these
actual petals and adds only points in disjoint own/target cores (or
uses uniformity padding from which disjoint choices can be selected).
Take the corresponding q full members. Their own cores are mutually
disjoint, as are their actual S_x. Each pairwise intersection therefore
requires a residual core-target slot of one endpoint. A residual slot
can target only ONE of the other disjoint cores, and hence accounts
for at most one pair. Consequently
 binom(q,2) <= sum_{x selected} d_x,
and in particular max_x d_x >=(q-1)/2.

This statement is NOT about arbitrary modifications of the actual
petal system: adding common actual points can destroy the matching,
but may also destroy Hall. It directly obstructs economical target
completion of the fixed factored reverse system.

Cloning every old point L times and replacing each inner occurrence by
the whole clone-pack does NOT fix this: the same proof gives one
self-petal per nonanchor cloned corepoint, so
 q=L*sum_i|X_i|-T.
In the regime |X_i|>=T-d with d=o(T), q is asymptotic to L*T^2.
Thus target completion needs maximum deficit Omega(L*T^2), not O(T*d)
or O(T*L). The tempting golden-ratio reverse recursion cannot be
implemented by this unmodified factored reverse architecture.
